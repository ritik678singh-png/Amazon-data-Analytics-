# Amazon-Style E-commerce Sales Analysis — Findings

## Dataset
100,000 synthetic transaction rows covering 2020-01-01 to 2024-12-29. This is not official Amazon data.

## Headline metrics
- Recorded transaction amount: $91,825,647.92
- Gross line value (Quantity × UnitPrice): $91,021,212.99
- Units sold: 300,140
- Average recorded amount per order: $918.26
- Unique customers: 43,233
- Delivered orders: 74.628%
- Cancelled plus returned orders: 6.077%

## Geography
The United States accounts for 70,058 orders and $64,310,048.50 recorded transaction amount. India accounts for 15,051 orders and $13,875,839.12.

## Category mix
Recorded transaction amounts are relatively close across the six categories, ranging from approximately $15.16M to $15.58M.

## Interpretation and limitations
- These are descriptive findings from synthetic data, not forecasts or causal conclusions.
- Recorded transaction amount is not audited net revenue.
- Gross line value is not profit.
- Cost of goods, operating expenses and margins are not provided.
- Product/category mappings contain apparent semantic inconsistencies and should be validated before making merchandising decisions.
